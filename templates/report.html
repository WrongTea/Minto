Before you submit a report

Please make sure that the content you are reporting actually violates Minto's community rules.

  • Do not submit false or misleading reports.
  • Do not report content simply because you dislike it.
  • Reports should be related to a possible violation of our community rules.
  • Do not use reports to harass or target other users.
  • Provide as much useful information as possible.

What should you include in your report?

Please provide enough information so our moderation team can clearly understand the situation and review the report.

  • Tell us what happened and clearly describe the problem.
  • Provide the username of the person you are reporting.
  • Include a direct link to the reported video, profile, or comment whenever possible.
  • Select the reason that best describes the violation.
  • If possible, attach a screenshot or other evidence that can help us review the situation.
  • Do not include false information or report someone simply because you dislike their content.
